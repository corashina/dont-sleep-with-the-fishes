# Items and their animations
In all of these animatons the player camera must remain in one position, it cannot move, it can only look at different things.
In all of these animations, first click on the item should make it move up in the air a little, then move towards the player like it's about to go to his hand.
Each item should have an item pickup animation by player before it shows how it's handled.

Food: Animation should show throwing the food at the corresponding event entity.

Bait: Animation should show throwing the bait at the corresponding event entity.

Duct Tape: Animation should show duct tape being stretched in hands by player to prepare for taping like in real life. It should have duct tape sound. After looking the animation should return them to hand.

Compass: Animation should show camera focus on the compass and show player looking in different directions on the ocean and then back at the compass again. After looking the animation should return them to hand.

Map: Animation should show the map being read by player. He should take it in front of him and start reading it.

Medkit: Animation should show throwing the bait at the corresponding event entity.

Binoculars: Animation should show player use binoculars as normal human. It should show a zoom in animation on the corresponding event entity. It should have two circles visible with black edges around it like they would in real life. After looking the animation should return them to hand.

Fishing Net: Animation should show fishnet net being thrown at the entity, fishes or corresponding event items. Use bigger, more visible net during throw instead of current model to better indicate. Net should be a simple rectangle net instead of current model, it should change its shape during and after throw to facilitate physics.

Bucket: In case of fishing or similar the animation should show bucket being used to fish out whatever is nearby in the water. In other cases the bucket should have the animation of putting it on the head and obstructing the view (protection from entity).

Flare Gun: Animation look like a normal flare gun shot. In case of no danger it should fire the gun into the air in hope of rescue. In case of danger and event entity it should fire the flare gun at the event entity. Shot should produce light, sound, knockback effect and the actual flare that goes in the air and shines at the sky. After a while the flare should fade away. Flare should be bright and visible. After looking the animation should return them to hand.

Anchor: Animation should have the anchor move to the side of the boat, outside of the boat. Then it should show the anchor dropping into the ocean like a normal anchor would work.

Bottled Paper:

Umbrella: Animation should have the player use the umbrella like a normal human. He should either put it on top of himself to protect from rain and similar weather or in front of him to protect from corresponding event entity.

Swim Ring: Animation should have the player throw the swim ring at the corresponding event entity.

Flashlight: Animation should have the player flash the flashlight at the corresponding event entity. It should produce light like a normal flashlight would do. It should flash a few times.

Harpoon Gun: Animation should have the player shoot the gun at the corresponding event entity. It should produce gunshot sound and have quick smoke burst out of the barrel. It should also have a knockback effect. After looking the animation should return them to hand.

Energy Bar: Animation should show throwing the bait at the corresponding event entity.
